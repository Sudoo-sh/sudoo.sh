# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. People contribute in their own time, from many countries and in their second or third language.
- Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual content, or publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, Yousef M. Y. Al Sabbah ([@Yosef-AlSabbah](https://github.com/Yosef-AlSabbah)), privately through the contact details on his GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who does, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
