# Getting Help

1. **Read the documentation first.** The [documentation index](docs/README.md) answers most "how do I...?" questions. The same documents are shown inside the panel under **Documentation**.
2. **Something fails on your server?** Look at the plain-language message and the technical log the panel shows next to it, then the *Troubleshooting* part of the [Administrator guide](docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md).
3. **Found a bug?** Open an issue with the **Bug report** form.
4. **Have an idea?** Open an issue with the **Feature request** form.
5. **Found a security problem?** Do not open an issue: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For a commercial licence, or help that does not fit an issue, contact the maintainer, Yousef M. Y. Al Sabbah ([@Yosef-AlSabbah](https://github.com/Yosef-AlSabbah)).

When you ask for help, never paste `.env` files, passwords, deploy keys or the links of instances that hold real data.
